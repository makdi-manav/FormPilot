Security: Your API key is stored locally in your browser's private chrome.storage.local. It is never logged, never transmitted to external servers, and never shared.

Free-Tier Notice: If you are using the free tier of Google AI Studio, Google may use prompt and response data to train and improve its products. Never submit sensitive, proprietary, confidential, or personally identifiable data.
